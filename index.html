📁 3. Discriminatory_Event_2_13_2025 🕐 timeline.docx
Hostile environment A legal standard under Title VI: a campus climate so permeated with discrimination that it effectively denies a student equal access to educational programs.
Pattern evidence Individual incidents that, taken together, demonstrate a recurring discriminatory practice by the institution — even where no single event is independently actionable.
SJP (Students for Justice in Palestine) A student organization placed under suspension by UCLA. SOLE and UCPD allowed SJP to proceed with a campus event during the suspension period, illustrating UCLA's selective enforcement in favor of anti-Israel groups and against Jewish-affiliated students.
UCPD UCLA Campus Police Department. Their failure to enforce SJP's suspension — while allowing SJP to conduct an event — is evidence of discriminatory application of university rules.
Title VI 42 U.S.C. § 2000d — federal law prohibiting national origin discrimination (including shared Jewish ancestry) at institutions receiving federal funding.
